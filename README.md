## 🚀 Key Features

1. **8 Authentic Folk-Art Designs (from the official PDF catalogue)**:
   - `01. Aaroh Signature`
   - `02. Veena & Mandala`
   - `03. Rooted`
   - `04. Warli Circle`
   - `05. Details (Peacock)`
   - `06. Tree of Life Deer`
   - `07. Made to Keep`
   - `08. Culture`

2. **Official Size & Page Pricing Matrix**:
   - **Compact (10 x 14 cm)**: 10 pgs = Rs. 199 | 15 pgs = Rs. 229 | 20 pgs = Rs. 249
   - **Large (14 x 17.5 cm)**: 10 pgs = Rs. 229 | 15 pgs = Rs. 249 | 20 pgs = Rs. 279

3. **Automatic Bulk Order Discounts**:
   - 10 – 19 diaries: **-Rs. 10 off** per diary
   - 20 – 49 diaries: **-Rs. 40 off** per diary
   - 50+ diaries: **-Rs. 54 off** per diary

4. **1-Tap WhatsApp Store Sharing**:
   - Click **"📲 Share Link on WhatsApp"** in the hero section to open a pre-formatted announcement message ready to send into your WhatsApp channel, community, or status!

5. **Deep-Linking for Individual Diary Promotion**:
   - Every product card has a **"🔗 Share"** button that generates a direct link:  
     `https://your-store.com/?design=01`
   - When a customer clicks that link in WhatsApp, the store opens and **automatically scrolls to and opens the order pop-up for that exact diary**!

6. **Full Shopping Bag**:
   - For customers wanting to order multiple different designs together in one single parcel.
